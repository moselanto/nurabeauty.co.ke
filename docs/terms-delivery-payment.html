Delivery

Where we deliver

We deliver to every county in Kenya. You can also collect your order free of charge from our studio at Stanbank House, 2nd Floor, Room 20, Nairobi CBD (Monday to Saturday, 9:00am to 7:30pm).

How we deliver and what it costs

The delivery fee depends on where you are and how you want your order sent. We confirm the exact fee before you pay.

  • Nairobi CBD: delivered by our rider for KSh 300.
  • Rest of Nairobi: rider delivery from KSh 300, depending on distance. Delivery within Nairobi is free on orders over KSh 10,000.
  • Countrywide by shuttle or bus parcel: the fee is the shuttle or bus company's sending charge from its Nairobi CBD office. If your shuttle or bus leaves from a stage outside the CBD (for example Eastleigh), a rider fee to take the parcel there is added to the sending cost.
  • Countrywide by courier: G4S, Wells Fargo or another courier that serves your town. The fee depends on your location and the courier you choose.
  • International: available on request. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Delivery times

  • Nairobi: same-day delivery for orders confirmed before 5:00pm, Monday to Saturday. Orders after 5:00pm go out the next working day.
  • Rest of Kenya: usually 1 to 3 working days after dispatch, depending on the courier and your town.
  • Times are estimates. Courier delays, weather, public holidays or incorrect contact details can affect them, and we will keep you updated on WhatsApp.

Receiving your order

  • Please give a correct name, phone number and delivery location. We are not responsible for delays or losses caused by wrong details.
  • For shuttle, bus and courier deliveries, we share the parcel or waybill number once your order is dispatched. You collect from the office with your ID, or receive it at your address where the service offers it.
  • Check your parcel when you receive it. Report any damage or a wrong item within 24 hours of receiving it, with photos, on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Once a parcel is handed to a shuttle, bus or courier service, delivery is that service's responsibility, and we will help you follow up any claim.

Payment

Accepted methods

M-Pesa and Visa/Mastercard (both through Paystack at checkout, including an M-Pesa prompt to your phone on WhatsApp orders), M-Pesa Buy Goods Till 1679269 (Nura Beauty & Crown), and bank transfer.

Pay on delivery: Nairobi CBD only

  • Pay on delivery is available only for deliveries within Nairobi CBD. You can pay the rider in cash or by M-Pesa when your order arrives.
  • Please have the full amount ready. If an order is refused at delivery without a valid reason, we may ask for payment in advance on future orders.

All other deliveries are paid before dispatch

  • Orders delivered outside Nairobi CBD, including the rest of Nairobi and countrywide, are paid in full, including the delivery fee, before we dispatch them.
  • We dispatch only once your payment is confirmed.

Studio pick-up

Orders collected at our studio can be paid in person in cash or by M-Pesa when you collect, or online in advance.

Lipa Mdogo Mdogo

Instalment orders follow the separate Lipa Mdogo Mdogo terms: a 50% deposit, with the balance due within 1 or 2 weeks. The wig is released only once it is fully paid.
