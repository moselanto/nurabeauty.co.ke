What makes a good work wig

  • Glueless: headband and closure wigs come off at night and are kind to your edges.
  • Neat texture: kinky straight, straight and bobs stay tidy all day.
  • Medium length: comfortable for long days and Nairobi heat.

Budget guide

Fibre closure and headband wigs start under KSh 3,000. If you wear a wig every day, a 100% human hair unit lasts about a year or more and can be restyled for weekends.
