How to choose

  • Length: 12 to 14 inches is easy everyday wear; 18 to 22 inches is long and glamorous. Curls shrink the visible length.
  • Texture: jerry curl and deep curl are full and bouncy; water curl is lighter and wet-look; a straight bob is the most polished.
  • Lace front: our human hair units are lace front for a natural hairline. We can install it for you at the studio.

How to tell genuine human hair

Human hair has a soft, low shine, moves naturally and takes heat without melting. At NURA every product name states its exact hair type, so a blend or fibre unit is never sold as human hair.
