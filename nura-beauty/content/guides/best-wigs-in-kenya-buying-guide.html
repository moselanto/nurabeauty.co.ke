Short answer: the best wig is the one that fits your budget, how often you wear it and how much styling you want to do. At NURA, heat-resistant fibre wigs are the most affordable, human-hair blend (semi-human) wigs sit in the middle, and 100% human hair wigs cost the most but last the longest. Headband and 4x4 closure wigs are the easiest to wear; lace front wigs give the most natural hairline.

Step 1: choose the hair type

  • 100% human hair: most natural, can be heat-styled and coloured, about a year or more with good care.
  • Human-hair blend (semi-human): softer than fibre at a lower price than human hair, about 6 to 9 months.
  • Heat-resistant fibre: the most affordable, styled out of the box, about 3 to 6 months.

Step 2: choose the construction

  • Headband wig: no lace and no glue; on in minutes. Best for beginners.
  • 4x4 closure wig: a small lace parting area, usually worn glueless.
  • Lace front (13x4 frontal): lace across the whole hairline for the most natural look; best fitted by a stylist.

Step 3: choose texture, length and colour

Kinky straight (Yaki) and afro textures look closest to natural African hair; water curl, jerry curl and deep curl give volume; bobs are neat and easy. Natural black (1B) and dark brown look the most natural.
