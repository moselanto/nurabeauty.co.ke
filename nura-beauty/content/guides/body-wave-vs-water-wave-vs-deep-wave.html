Short answer: body wave has big, loose S-waves and is the easiest to maintain; water wave has smaller, wet-look ripples that look best when refreshed with water and leave-in; deep wave has tighter, fuller waves with lots of volume; jerry curl has small springy curls with the most volume. The tighter the pattern, the more moisture and gentle detangling it needs.

Care tips for curly textures

  • Detangle from the ends up with fingers or a wide-tooth comb, never a fine brush when dry.
  • Use a light leave-in and a curl serum or curl keeper to revive the pattern.
  • Wash human hair curls every 2 to 3 weeks with sulphate-free shampoo and condition well.
  • Fibre curls: cool water only, air dry on a stand, no high heat.
