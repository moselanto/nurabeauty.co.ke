Short answer: for a wedding, choose a 100% human hair or high-quality lace front wig for the most natural look in photos, buy it at least 3 to 4 weeks before the day, do a trial fitting, and book installation for the morning of the wedding or the day before.

Timeline

  1. 4+ weeks before: choose your style and buy the wig.
  2. 2 to 3 weeks before: trial fitting and any colour or cut.
  3. Day before or morning of: installation and styling.

What to choose

  • Human hair for heat styling and a natural finish.
  • Lace front for a natural hairline in close-up photos.
