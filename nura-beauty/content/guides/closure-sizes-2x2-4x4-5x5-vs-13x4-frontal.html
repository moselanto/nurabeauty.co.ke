Short answer: the numbers are the lace size in inches. A 2x2 closure gives a small middle part and is the cheapest; a 4x4 closure is the most popular all-rounder with room for middle and slight side parts; a 5x5 closure gives a wider parting area; a 13x4 frontal has lace from ear to ear for the most natural hairline and any parting, but needs more skill to install. Bigger lace costs more.

Which should you choose?

  • First lace wig or busy schedule: 4x4 closure.
  • Tight budget: 2x2 closure or a headband wig (no lace).
  • You love side parts: 5x5 closure or 13x6 frontal.
  • Ponytails, pulled-back styles, events: 13x4 frontal, installed at the studio.
