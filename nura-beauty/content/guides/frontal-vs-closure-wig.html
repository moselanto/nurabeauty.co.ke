Short answer: a lace front (frontal) wig has lace along the whole front hairline, so you can wear it pulled back and part it almost anywhere for the most natural look. A closure wig (such as 4x4) has a smaller lace square at the top, giving a natural parting with less lace to cut and lower maintenance, usually at a lower price.

Quick comparison

HairlineNatural across the frontNatural at the parting
