Short answer: a glueless install holds the wig with an elastic band, combs or a wig grip, so you can take it off daily and it is kinder to your edges. A glued (bonded) install uses lace glue or tape for the flattest, most "melted" hairline and holds for one to three weeks, but needs careful removal and gives your edges less rest. Choose glueless for everyday comfort; choose glued for events, photos and very windy or active days.

Tips for either install

  • Start with a clean, dry hairline and a flat base (cornrows or a wig cap).
  • Glueless: adjust the band so it is snug but not tight, and add a wig grip for extra hold.
  • Glued: do a small patch test first; use thin layers and let each dry until clear.
  • Always remove glue with a bond remover, never by pulling the lace.
  • Give your edges a break between glued installs.
