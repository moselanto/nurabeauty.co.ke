Short answer: a glueless wig stays on with an adjustable band, combs or clips instead of adhesive, so you can put it on and take it off in minutes without damaging your edges. Headband wigs and most closure wigs can be worn glueless.

Why choose glueless

  • Gentler on your hairline and skin.
  • Quick to put on and remove daily.
  • No glue to buy or clean off.

Good glueless options

  • Headband wigs: the easiest of all.
  • 4x4 closure wigs: natural parting with little lace.
