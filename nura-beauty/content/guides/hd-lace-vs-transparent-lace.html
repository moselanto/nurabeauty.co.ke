Short answer: HD lace is thinner and melts into almost any skin tone with little or no tint, so it looks the most invisible, but it tears more easily and costs more. Transparent lace is slightly thicker and stronger, lasts longer with regular wear, and usually needs a little lace tint on deeper skin tones. For everyday wear in Kenya, transparent lace is the practical choice; for events and photos, HD lace looks best.

How to make either lace look natural

  • Cut the lace close to the hairline, leaving a tiny strip, never into the hair.
  • Tint the lace with a lace tint spray in a shade close to your skin.
  • Use a melting spray or a thin layer of glue, tied down with a band for 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Lay baby hair lightly with edge gel.

Care

  • Remove glue with a bond remover, never by pulling the lace.
  • Wash the lace gently; do not scrub.
  • HD lace in particular should be stored on a wig stand, not folded.
