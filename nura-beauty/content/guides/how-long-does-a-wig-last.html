Short answer: with regular wear and good care, a 100% human hair wig lasts about a year or more, a semi-human (blend) wig about 6 to 9 months, and a heat-resistant fibre wig about 3 to 6 months. Wearing it less often, gentle washing and proper storage make any wig last longer.

What shortens a wig's life

  • Sleeping in the wig, which causes friction and tangles.
  • Too much heat, especially on fibre and blend units.
  • Washing too often or with harsh shampoo.
  • Brushing from the roots instead of from the ends.
  • Storing it loose instead of on a stand or in a satin bag.

Simple habits that help

  1. Detangle from the ends up with a wide-tooth comb.
  2. Wash with cool water and sulphate-free shampoo; wash human hair about every 6 to 8 weeks of regular wear.
  3. Air dry on a stand, never wring.
  4. Rotate two wigs if you wear one daily.
  5. Book a wig revamp when it starts to look tired.
