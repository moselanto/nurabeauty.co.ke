Short answer: wash a fibre wig only when it needs it (about every 10 to 15 wears) in cool water with a mild shampoo, detangle gently from the ends with a wide-tooth comb, air dry on a stand and use low heat only. Store it on a stand or in a satin bag away from direct sun.

How to wash a fibre wig

  1. Comb out tangles from the ends up before washing.
  2. Soak in cool water with a little mild or sulphate-free shampoo for 5 minutes.
  3. Rinse in cool water; do not rub or twist.
  4. Press out water with a towel.
  5. Air dry on a wig stand; never use a hot blow dryer.

Heat and styling

Heat-resistant fibre tolerates low heat only. Test a small hidden section first. For a bigger restyle, book a wig revamp.

Storage

Keep it on a stand or in a satin bag, away from perfume sprays, heat and direct sun.
