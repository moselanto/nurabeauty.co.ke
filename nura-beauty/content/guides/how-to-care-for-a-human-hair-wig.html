Short answer: wash a human hair wig about every 6 to 8 weeks of regular wear with sulphate-free shampoo, condition every time, air dry on a stand, always use a heat protectant before styling, and store it in a satin bag. With this routine a human hair wig can be worn for a year or more.

Washing step by step

  1. Detangle from the ends up.
  2. Wash in lukewarm water with sulphate-free shampoo, smoothing downwards.
  3. Condition generously, avoiding the lace and knots.
  4. Rinse, blot with a towel and air dry on a stand.

Heat and colour

Human hair can be straightened, curled and coloured. Use a heat protectant and moderate heat. For colour changes, book a revamp so the lace stays healthy.
