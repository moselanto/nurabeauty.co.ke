Short answer: a fibre wig costs less to buy, but a human hair wig often costs less per wear because it lasts much longer. Divide the price by the number of days you will wear it: a KSh 2,500 fibre wig worn about 120 times costs about KSh 21 per wear; a KSh 12,000 human hair wig worn about 400 times costs about KSh 30 per wear, but looks more natural and can be restyled. If you wear a wig most days for a year or more, human hair is usually better value.

What else to count

  • Care products: human hair needs sulphate-free shampoo, conditioner and heat protectant; fibre needs gentle products and cool water.
  • Revamps: a wash and revamp (from KSh 1,500 at NURA) can add months to a human hair wig.
  • Styling freedom: human hair can be curled, straightened and coloured; fibre holds one style.
  • Spreading the cost: Lipa Mdogo Mdogo lets you pay a 50% deposit and the rest within 1 or 2 weeks.
