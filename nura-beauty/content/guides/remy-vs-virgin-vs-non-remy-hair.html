Short answer: remy means the hair cuticles were kept in one direction, so it tangles less; virgin means the hair has never been chemically processed; raw is unprocessed hair from a single donor; non-remy hair has mixed cuticles and is usually acid-treated and coated, so it tangles once the coating washes out. Grades like 8A, 10A or 12A are seller labels with no independent standard, so judge the hair, not the number.

How to judge a human hair wig

  • Run your fingers from tip to root: aligned cuticles feel slightly rougher going up.
  • Check the ends: double drawn hair stays thick to the tips.
  • Burn test on two hidden strands: human hair burns to fine ash and smells like burnt hair; fibre melts into a hard bead.
  • Be wary of very cheap "100% human hair": a full human hair wig in Kenya normally costs from about KSh 5,000 for short styles.

Sources: American Hair Loss Association on hair types and grades; The Standard (Evewoman) on the burn test.
