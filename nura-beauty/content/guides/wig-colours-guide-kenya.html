Short answer: natural black (called 1B) and dark brown look the most natural on most Kenyan skin tones. Burgundy, copper and honey shades add warmth and glow on dark skin, and an ombre with a dark root gives you colour while keeping a natural-looking parting.

Colour guide

  • 1B / natural black: softer than jet black and the most natural choice.
  • Dark brown: softens your features and looks warm in sunlight.
  • Burgundy: rich and bold, great for events and birthdays.
  • Honey blonde and highlights: bright and fun; a dark root keeps it natural.
  • Ombre: dark root fading to a lighter shade.

Can I dye my wig?

100% human hair wigs can be coloured, best by a professional at a studio. Blend wigs are not recommended for colouring and fibre wigs cannot be dyed, so choose the colour you want from the start.
