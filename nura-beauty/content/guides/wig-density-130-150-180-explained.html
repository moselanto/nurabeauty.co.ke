Short answer: density is how much hair is on the wig cap. 130% looks light and natural, close to most people's own hair; 150% is the most popular everyday fullness; 180% looks full and glamorous; 200% and above is very voluminous and heavier. Higher density uses more hair, so it costs more and weighs more.

DensityLookBest forWeight and price
130%Light, naturalOffice, first wig, short stylesLightest, lowest
150%Natural fullnessEveryday wear, most stylesMedium
180%Full and glamorousEvents, long and curly stylesHeavier, higher
200%+Very voluminousStatement and stage looksHeaviest, highest

How to choose

  • Short bobs and pixies look most natural at 130% to 150%.
  • Long straight or body wave wigs look fuller at 150% to 180%.
  • Curly textures (water wave, deep wave, jerry curl) already look full, so 150% to 180% is plenty.
  • Hot weather and long days: lower density is cooler and lighter.
