Short answer: book a morning appointment online at our studio in Imenti House, Moi Avenue, Nairobi CBD. A deposit secures your slot and is deducted from your bill. Come with clean, dry hair, arrive on time (there is a 15-minute grace period), and our stylist will fit, cut the lace, and style your wig.

How booking works

  1. Choose your service and a morning time on the booking page.
  2. Pay the deposit by M-Pesa and enter the code.
  3. We confirm on WhatsApp.

Services

Fitting, installation, revamp and styling. See current prices on the installation page. Video and WhatsApp consultations are free.

How to prepare

  • Wash and dry your hair; braid flat if possible.
  • Bring the wig if you bought it elsewhere.
  • Tell us about any scalp sensitivity.
