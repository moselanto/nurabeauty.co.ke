Short answer: body wave has big, loose S-waves; water wave (water curl) has smaller, wet-look curls with more volume; jerry curl is a springy, defined curl; kinky straight (Yaki) is straight with a slightly coarse texture like blow-dried African hair. A 12 inch wig sits around the shoulders and a 22 inch wig towards the bust; curls shrink the visible length.

Textures

  • Kinky straight (Yaki): the most natural blend with African hair.
  • Afro kinky and coily curl: a natural African look with volume.
  • Water curl: light, wet-look curls.
  • Jerry curl and deep curl: full, bouncy, glamorous.
  • Bubble curl: big, playful curls.
  • Straight and bone straight: sleek and polished.

Lengths

  • 8 to 10 inches: bob, chin to jaw.
  • 12 inches: around the shoulders.
  • 14 inches: collarbone.
  • 16 inches: just below the collarbone.
  • 18 inches: mid-chest.
  • 20 to 22 inches: towards the bust or below.
