What is a semi-human wig?

A semi-human wig is made from a blend of human hair and synthetic fibre. The human hair gives softer movement and a more natural shine than fibre alone. The fibre helps the wig hold its style and keeps the price lower than a full human hair unit.

Who a semi-human wig suits

  • You want a more natural look than fibre without paying for full human hair.
  • You like a style that stays in place with little daily work.
  • You are trying a new length or texture before investing in human hair.
  • You want a dependable everyday wig to rotate with a human hair unit.

How to care for a semi-human wig

  • Wash in cool or lukewarm water with a sulphate-free shampoo.
  • Use a light conditioner and rinse well.
  • Detangle gently with a wide-tooth comb, starting at the ends.
  • Let it air dry on a wig stand.
  • Use low heat only, and follow the guidance on the product page.
  • Store it on a stand or in a satin bag, and sleep in a satin bonnet.

Semi-human wig FAQs

Are semi-human wigs real hair? Partly. They blend human hair with fibre, so they are not 100% human hair.

Can I straighten or curl a semi-human wig? Yes, with low heat. Check the product page for the safe temperature.

Can I dye a semi-human wig? We do not recommend it. The fibre does not take colour the way human hair does. Choose a human hair wig if you want to colour it.

Is a semi-human wig good for beginners? Yes. A blend closure wig is affordable, natural-looking and easy to wear.
