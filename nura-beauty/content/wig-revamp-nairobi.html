Wigs we revamp

  • Human hair wigs: full wash, conditioning, heat styling, curls restored and colour on request.
  • Human hair blend (semi-human) wigs: wash, condition and gentle styling with low heat.
  • High fibre wigs: wash, detangle and refresh. We only use heat on heat-resistant fibre, at a low setting.

Signs your wig needs a revamp

  • Tangling or matting at the nape
  • Dryness, frizz or a dull look
  • Curls that have dropped or lost their shape
  • Product build-up on the hair or lace
  • Shedding or a loose lace, where a re-lace may help

How it works

  1. Send us a photo of your wig on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the price and when to bring it in.
  3. Drop it at the studio, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm.
  4. We message you when it is ready to collect. Want it installed too? Book an installation for the same visit.

Keep it fresh after your revamp

  • Store it on a wig stand or in a satin bag.
  • Sleep in a satin bonnet or scarf.
  • Use light products and avoid heavy oils near the lace.
  • Book a wash and revamp every 6 to 8 weeks of regular wear.
