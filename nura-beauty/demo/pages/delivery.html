How your NURA order reaches you — fast, tracked, and beautifully packaged. For full fees and timelines see Shipping & Delivery.

Same-day Nairobi

Order a ready-to-wear unit before 2:00 PM and receive it the same day within Nairobi via our rider. Free on orders over KES 10,000.

Countrywide

We deliver to every county via Fargo Courier and G4S, typically within 1–3 business days. You will get an SMS with your waybill number to track your parcel.

Pickup points

Choose Pick Mtaani at checkout to collect from a convenient Nairobi pickup point at a lower fee, or select Store Pickup to collect from our studio.

International & diaspora

We ship worldwide via DHL and FedEx, priced in USD with full tracking, in 3–10 business days. Duties and taxes at destination are the recipient's responsibility.

Tracking your delivery

Track any order from Track Order or your NURA Circle account. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
