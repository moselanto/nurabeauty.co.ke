Everything you need to know about NURA wigs, delivery, payment and care. Still have a question? Chat with us on WhatsApp.

Are NURA wigs real human hair?

Yes. Our premium units are verified human hair with a provenance certificate and written guarantee. We never sell synthetic hair as human hair.

How do I choose the right wig for me?

Book a free virtual consultation, take our hair quiz, or try the AI Wig Finder — upload a selfie and we recommend the best style for your face shape, skin tone and lifestyle. You can also visit our Nairobi studio for a fitting.

What lengths, textures and colours are available?

Most units come in 10"–30" lengths, densities from 130%–200%, textures from straight to afro curly, and colours from natural black to blonde, ombré, burgundy and 613. Each variation has its own price and availability shown on the product page.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa (STK push, Buy Goods/Till, or Pochi), Visa/Mastercard, PayPal, and pay-on-delivery in Nairobi. For units above KES 15,000 you can use NURA Flex: a 50% M-Pesa deposit to begin, balance before delivery, or split over instalments. Lipa Later / BNPL is available on eligible orders.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day within Nairobi (order before 2:00 PM), 1–3 business days countrywide, and 3–10 business days internationally with tracking. See Shipping & Delivery.

Can you install the wig for me?

Yes. Book installation at our studio or, for VIP clients, at home. We also offer styling, colouring, wash & revamp and repairs. See Installation.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — premium units carry a written longevity guarantee (up to 6 months on custom units). See Warranty for full terms.

Can I return or exchange a wig?

Unworn, unaltered units can be returned within 48 hours of delivery. Worn, cut or installed units cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. See Returns & Refunds.

Do you ship to the diaspora?

Yes, worldwide via DHL/FedEx, priced in USD with full tracking. Contact us for a quote.

Do you sell to salons and businesses?

Yes — see Bulk & Corporate Orders for wholesale pricing and corporate gifting.

How do I care for my wig?

Every unit ships with a care card and a QR code to a care video. Follow the wash, condition and storage routine to keep your unit looking new. Join The NURA Circle for care reminders and scheduled revamps.
