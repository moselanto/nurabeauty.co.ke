Pickup from our studio

Prefer to collect and try on in person? Choose Store Pickup at checkout and collect from our Nairobi studio during opening hours. We will notify you when your order is ready.

International & diaspora shipping

We ship worldwide for our diaspora clients, priced in USD with full DHL/FedEx tracking. Import duties and taxes in the destination country are the recipient's responsibility. Contact us for a shipping quote before ordering large or bulk consignments.
