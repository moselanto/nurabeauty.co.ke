Track your order

Once your order is confirmed you will receive an SMS and email with your order number (e.g. #NURA-1042) and a tracking link. You can also track here at any time:

  • By account: Log in to The NURA Circle and open Orders to see every purchase, its status and courier tracking.
  • By WhatsApp: Send us your order number and we will send you a live update, usually within minutes during business hours.
  • By courier: Countrywide parcels ship via Fargo Courier / G4S and same-day Nairobi via our rider or Pick Mtaani. Your dispatch SMS includes the courier waybill number.

Delivery timelines

  • Ready-to-wear units: Same-day within Nairobi (order before 2:00 PM), 1–3 business days countrywide.
  • Custom & bespoke builds: 3–7 business days as each unit is sewn to order.
  • International / diaspora: 3–10 business days via DHL/FedEx with full tracking.
