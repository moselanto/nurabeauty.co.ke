Every premium NURA unit is verified human hair and ships with a written longevity guarantee and a provenance certificate. We stand behind our craft.

What the NURA guarantee covers

  • Verified human hair: Your unit is genuine human hair as described — never a synthetic substitute sold as human hair.
  • Craftsmanship: Wefts, knots and lace are hand-finished to last. We cover defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
  • Longevity: With proper care, our premium units are built to last 12+ months of regular wear.

What is not covered

  • Normal wear, or damage from heat, harsh chemicals, colouring or cutting done outside NURA.
  • Poor maintenance, improper storage, or damage from adhesives.
  • Tangling or dryness caused by skipping the recommended care routine.

Your provenance certificate

Premium and bespoke units are numbered and ship with a certificate confirming origin and the guarantee terms. Keep it — you can also view your warranty certificates any time in The NURA Circle portal.

Making a claim

  1. Contact us with your order number and provenance certificate number.
  2. Send clear photos or a short video showing the issue.
  3. We assess and, where covered, repair, replace or restore the unit. Many issues (re-lacing, re-knotting, revamps) are fixed by our atelier.

We aim to resolve every valid claim quickly and discreetly.
